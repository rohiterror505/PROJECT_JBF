📝 Lucky Draw Rules

  1. Eligibility & odds. All 9999 coupons (numbers 0001–9999) are eligible for the draw — whether sold or unsold. Each coupon carries an equal 1-in-9999 chance of being drawn.
    • Online (0001–4999): sold via the website. Buyer name and contact are recorded digitally at the time of purchase.
    • Offline (5000–9999): sold as physical paper coupons (booklets of 10, booklets of 5, or single coupons). These are bearer instruments — no buyer details are recorded. Whoever physically holds the winning coupon at draw time may claim the prize.
  2. Each coupon costs ₹100 and is sold in sets of 1, 5 or 10.
  3. The draw is conducted electronically. The system randomly picks 50 distinct winning coupon numbers from all 9999 coupons — no coupon can win twice.
  4. If an unsold coupon is drawn, the winner is marked UNSOLD — the prize is unclaimed and there is no re-draw. (This preserves exact 1-in-9999 odds for every coupon.)
  5. Online and offline coupons are entered in the same pool with no separate quota — online buyers get the same odds as offline holders.
  6. Winners are revealed in the order: Consolation D → C → B → A, then 10th Prize → 1st Prize (grand finale).
  7. The draw is performed live on screen at the venue. Results are saved permanently once conducted and cannot be re-run until the organisers clear them.
  8. Sold winners must have their coupon number and identity verified before their prize is released. See the claim process below for doorstep delivery details.
  9. All prizes are delivered to the winner's doorstep after verification — no need to collect in person at the venue.
  10. The decision of the organisers is final and binding on all contestants.

⚖ How the Draw is Fair

  • Uniform random selection. A certified electronic random-number generator draws 50 distinct numbers uniformly from the full 0001–9999 pool. Every coupon — online or offline, sold or unsold — has the same 1-in-9999 probability.
  • One pool, both channels. Online coupons (0001–4999) and offline coupons (5000–9999) compete in the same draw. There is no separate quota for either channel.
  • No double wins. Each drawn number is removed from the pool, so a single coupon cannot win more than one prize.
  • Live & transparent. The draw runs on screen at the venue, so contestants can witness every winning number being drawn in real time.
  • Permanent, tamper-proof results. Once the draw is complete, results are saved and locked. They cannot be re-run or altered except by an explicit organisers' reset.
  • No re-draw for unsold numbers. If an unsold coupon is drawn, the prize is declared unclaimed rather than re-rolling. This keeps the odds mathematically exact for every coupon that was sold.
  • Bearer coupons treated equally. Offline coupons are not tied to a buyer name, but their numbers sit in the same pool and follow the same random process as online coupons.
