📝 Lucky Draw Rules

  1. ALL 9999 coupons (numbers 1–9999) are eligible for the draw — whether sold or unsold. Each coupon has a 1-in-9999 chance of being drawn.
  2. Each coupon costs ₹100 and is sold in sets of 1, 5 or 10.
  3. The draw is conducted electronically. The system randomly picks 50 distinct winning coupon numbers from all 9999 coupons — no coupon can win twice.
  4. If an unsold coupon is drawn, the winner is marked UNSOLD — the prize is unclaimed and there is no re-draw.
  5. Winners are revealed in the order: Consolation D → C → B → A, then 10th Prize → 1st Prize (grand finale).
  6. Results are saved permanently once the draw is conducted and cannot be re-run until the organisers clear them.
  7. Sold winners must present the winning coupon (or matching coupon-number proof) to claim their prize.
  8. Prizes are distributed at the venue on draw day. Please carry valid identification.
  9. The decision of the organisers is final and binding on all contestants.
